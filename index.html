About

I am a doctoral researcher at the University of Amsterdam with Dr. Cameron Brick and Prof. Dr. Frenk van Harreveld studying the psychological dimensions of sustainable and just transitions. My core projects are about reducing clothing consumption and enhancing support for international climate finance. I use field experiments, discrete choice paradigms, and consequential behavior measures to emulate real-world settings and strengthen the practical relevance of the research. I also teach on social psychology and sustainability and consult on behavior change interventions.

Research

Three connected questions run through my work: why we consume, who pays for climate action, and how to measure both well enough to act on.

  • 01

    Consumption reduction

    What motivates and enables clothing overconsumption, and which interventions effectively reduce it?

  • 02

    Climate finance

    How do people think about global climate funding, and can support for reparations be strengthened?

  • 03

    Accurate measurement

    How can we track consumption in ways that link cleanly to environmental impacts, and how can we get a firm grasp on preferences?
